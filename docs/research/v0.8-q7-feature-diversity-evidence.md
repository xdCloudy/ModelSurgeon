# v0.8 Q7 feature-diversity acquisition evidence

## Result

The 75% predictive-uncertainty / 25% feature-diversity policy did not improve
sample efficiency consistently across the three measured model campaigns. It tied
the target budget on SmolLM2-360M, was inconclusive on SmolLM2-135M, and needed
more experiments on Qwen2.5-0.5B than either control. This is a negative result
for the preregistered hybrid policy; it is retained as evidence rather than used
to promote the policy as a default.

On Qwen2.5-0.5B, the hybrid policy required 25.6 more experiments than random
and 64 more than uncertainty-only on average among paired target-reaching seeds.
Its AULC was slightly higher than either control, showing why AULC and target
budget are both retained. SmolLM2-360M reached the target at the same budget in
all arms; its AULC advantage over uncertainty did not beat random. SmolLM2-135M
had only one target-reaching seed per arm, with no common paired reaches, so its
mean among reaches is descriptive and no paired reduction is defined.

## Protocol

- Three immutable campaigns: SmolLM2-135M, SmolLM2-360M, and Qwen2.5-0.5B, each
  with a 409/52/51 component-held-out train/validation/test split.
- Each of five seeds (11, 23, 37, 53, 71) gives all strategies the same stratified
  16-example initial labeled set and the same budget grid: 16, 32, 64, 128, 256,
  and 384.
- At each step, models are fitted on only revealed training labels. Validation
  and test partitions never enter candidate acquisition. All strategies are
  measured on the same held-out test set.
- Hybrid acquisition reserves 75% of each addition batch for Bernoulli predictive
  uncertainty and 25% for normalized feature/topology farthest-first diversity.
  Previously labeled candidates seed the center set. Candidate features use the
  source-only preprocessor fitted for that round and pre-mutation metadata.
- The common target is held-out ROC-AUC >= 0.90. The report retains all 45 curves,
  all budget points, paired target-reaching deltas, measured selected-candidate
  evaluation times, and newly selected candidate ID digests.
- GPU-hour values sum recorded CUDA mutation-evaluation wall time. They do not
  measure classifier fit time, end-to-end campaign time, energy, or inference speed.

This is an offline replay of already measured mutation labels. It evaluates
candidate-selection sample efficiency for the Meta-Surgeon training dataset; it
does not show that structural pruning produces smaller or faster models or
preserves end-user task quality.
