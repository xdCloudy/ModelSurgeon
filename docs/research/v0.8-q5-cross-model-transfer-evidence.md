# v0.8 Q5 unseen-model transfer evidence

## Result

A gradient-profile LightGBM trained on SmolLM2-135M and Qwen2.5-0.5B
transferred successfully to a completely unseen SmolLM2-360M checkpoint. On the
51 component-held-out target candidates, cross-model classification reached 1.0
AUC and 1.0 precision@10. The within-model comparator reached 0.9524 AUC and 1.0
precision@10.

| Metric | Within-model | Cross-model | Cross degradation (95% CI) |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| AUC | 0.95238 | 1.00000 | -0.04762 (-0.13341–0.00000) |
| precision@10 | 1.00000 | 1.00000 | 0.00000 (0.00000–0.00000) |
| MAE | 0.00863 | 0.01039 | +0.00176 (-0.00111–+0.00429) |
| RMSE | 0.01159 | 0.01275 | +0.00116 (-0.00234–+0.00481) |

The cross-model classifier ranked this target better than the within-model
comparator in this single fold, while its probability calibration was materially
worse: calibration error increased from 0.1451 to 0.2920. Regression errors rose
slightly, but both paired confidence intervals include zero. These results support
ranking transfer for this held-out checkpoint; they do not support a claim that
cross-model probabilities are calibrated.

## Zero-target-example protocol

- The source fit uses 818 training rows and 104 validation rows: 409/52 from
  SmolLM2-135M and 409/52 from Qwen2.5-0.5B. Source test partitions are discarded.
- SmolLM2-360M contributes no training row, validation row, fitted numeric mean or
  scale, or learned categorical vocabulary. Its first exposure is inference on its
  51 test rows after both preprocessing and LightGBM fitting are complete.
- The target's Llama family is represented by SmolLM2-135M in the sources, while
  Qwen supplies a second architecture family. The evaluator rejects a target
  identity present among the sources and rejects an unrepresented target family.
- The classifier preprocessor is serialized in the report, including all 33
  source-fitted numeric transforms and categorical vocabularies. Its canonical
  SHA-256 is
  `1ade230124a6e35d7082beced87505e40715a164776744bfb985561ebf4bcec7`.
- Within-model comparison uses SmolLM2-360M's normal 409/52 train/validation split
  and exactly the same 51 target test examples. Paired bootstrap resampling uses
  identical target groups for both predictions.

Both paths use the Q3 static-plus-activation-plus-gradient feature profile, the
same ΔPPL <= 0.01 safe threshold, seed 42, split seed 43, and 1,000 grouped
bootstrap repetitions.

## Validity boundary

This is one unseen checkpoint, not leave-one-model-out evidence across the full
ladder. The target shares the Llama family and calibration corpus with one source,
and gradients and labels still derive from the same short calibration text. Q6
must broaden this to multiple held-out models and families, explicitly recording
schema and transfer failures. As in Q4, outcomes are independently measured
single-channel masks rather than simultaneous multi-channel surgery.
