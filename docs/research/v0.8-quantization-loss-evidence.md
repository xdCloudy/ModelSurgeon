# v0.8 surgery and requantization loss evidence

## Result

A matched-region experiment separates high-precision surgery loss, codec
requantization loss, and their interaction across 27 real MLP tensors from three
model families/sizes. Each arm operates on the same 4,096 sampled weights. Surgery
zeros the same aligned 1-in-16 positions before the combined arm is requantized.

| Codec | Requantization MSE (95% CI) | Surgery MSE (95% CI) | Combined MSE (95% CI) | Interaction MSE (95% CI) |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Q4_K | 1.516e-4 (1.108e-4, 1.899e-4) | 1.416e-3 (1.024e-3, 1.798e-3) | 1.562e-3 (1.117e-3, 1.962e-3) | -5.861e-6 (-1.514e-5, 2.459e-6) |
| Q5_K | 3.605e-5 (2.653e-5, 4.489e-5) | 1.416e-3 (1.055e-3, 1.775e-3) | 1.450e-3 (1.052e-3, 1.833e-3) | -1.931e-6 (-4.686e-6, 6.796e-7) |
| Q6_K | 8.036e-6 (5.686e-6, 1.026e-5) | 1.416e-3 (1.017e-3, 1.790e-3) | 1.423e-3 (1.015e-3, 1.796e-3) | -7.596e-7 (-9.575e-7, -5.581e-7) |
| Q8_0 | 7.532e-7 (5.499e-7, 9.556e-7) | 1.416e-3 (1.015e-3, 1.779e-3) | 1.417e-3 (1.003e-3, 1.792e-3) | -6.524e-8 (-8.392e-8, -4.629e-8) |

Requantization error falls monotonically with precision. The interaction is small
relative to the surgery main effect: its magnitude is 0.41% of surgery MSE for Q4_K,
0.14% for Q5_K, 0.054% for Q6_K, and 0.0046% for Q8_0. Q4_K and Q5_K intervals
include zero; Q6_K and Q8_0 show a small negative interaction. Thus the combined
weight-space loss is approximately additive for this fixed surgery, with a slight
sub-additive overlap at the higher-precision codecs.

## Protocol

- Models are revision-pinned SmolLM2-135M, SmolLM2-360M, and Qwen2.5-0.5B.
- Gate, up, and down projection weights are sampled from the first, middle, and
  final transformer layers: nine tensors per model and 110,592 weights total.
- The untouched control is encoded and decoded directly. The surgery-only arm
  zeros indexes `0, 16, 32, ...` in float32 sample space. The combined arm encodes
  and decodes that exact edited vector. No arm changes its tensor or index set.
- Per-tensor MSE is computed against the original high-precision sample. Main
  effects and interaction (`combined - requantization - surgery`) are averaged over
  tensors. Intervals are 1,000-repetition paired tensor bootstraps with seed 42.
- The runner records requested and resolved model revisions plus a SHA-256 digest
  for every sampled tensor, making the aligned input regions auditable.

## Validity boundary

This is a codec-local weight-quality decomposition, not an end-to-end perplexity or
latency claim. It samples selected MLP tensors, uses one deterministic zeroing
pattern, and treats tensors as the bootstrap unit. Correlation within models may
make the intervals optimistic, while other surgery operators can interact
differently with block scaling. Model-level quality conclusions require matched
full-model mutations and evaluation on held-out text.
