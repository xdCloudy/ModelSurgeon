# v0.8 Q7 multi-model active-learning evidence

## Result

Uncertainty acquisition reduced the experiments needed to reach 0.90 held-out AUC
on Qwen2.5-0.5B, but the benefit did not generalize across all three models. Qwen
needed 76.8 experiments on average versus 115.2 for paired seeded random runs, a
38.4-experiment reduction. SmolLM2-360M tied at 64 experiments. SmolLM2-135M
reached the target in only one active and one random seed—and not in the same
seed—so no paired reduction is defined.

| Model | Strategy | Target reaches | Mean experiments among reaches | Mean measured GPU hours among reaches | Mean AULC |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| SmolLM2-135M | active uncertainty | 1/5 | 128.0 | 0.000803 | 0.8337 |
|  | seeded random | 1/5 | 64.0 | 0.000423 | 0.8236 |
| SmolLM2-360M | active uncertainty | 5/5 | 64.0 | 0.000448 | 0.9196 |
|  | seeded random | 5/5 | 64.0 | 0.000459 | 0.9312 |
| Qwen2.5-0.5B | active uncertainty | 5/5 | 76.8 | 0.000476 | 0.8691 |
|  | seeded random | 5/5 | 115.2 | 0.000701 | 0.8702 |

On Qwen, the paired mean savings are 38.4 experiments and 0.000225 GPU hours
(0.812 seconds of measured CUDA evaluation wall time). On SmolLM2-360M, the
experiment saving is zero; the 0.0000107-hour timing difference comes only from
the selected candidates having slightly different measured runtimes. Despite the
Qwen target-reaching benefit, random has slightly higher mean AULC on Qwen and
SmolLM2-360M. This study therefore does not support a model-general claim that the
current uncertainty policy is more sample-efficient than random.

## Equal-budget replay protocol

- Each model uses its existing 409/52/51 component-held-out train, validation, and
  test split. The fixed validation and test partitions never enter acquisition.
- For each seed (11, 23, 37, 53, 71), active and random begin with exactly the same
  stratified 16-example labeled set. Budgets are 16, 32, 64, 128, 256, and 384.
- At every budget, a fresh LightGBM classifier is fit on only the acquired training
  labels and source-fitted preprocessing, then evaluated on the unchanged 51-row
  test set. Active adds candidates nearest probability 0.5; random follows its
  fixed seeded permutation.
- The common target is held-out ROC-AUC >= 0.90. The initial exploratory 0.80 target
  was rejected because every strategy and seed had already reached it by the old
  64-example starting budget; it could not measure reduction.
- GPU hours are the sum of the selected candidates' recorded CUDA experiment
  `evaluate` wall times divided by 3,600. They are measured allocated-GPU wall-hour
  proxies for mutation evaluation, not power-integrated energy and not end-to-end
  campaign time. Fixed feature collection and CPU LightGBM fitting are excluded.
- AULC is the normalized trapezoidal area over the identical 16–384 experiment grid.
  Target-reaching counts retain non-reaches rather than averaging them away.

All predictors use the Q3 static-plus-activation-plus-gradient feature profile and
the ΔPPL <= 0.01 safe label.

## Validity boundary

This is an offline replay: every label had already been measured, although each
policy is only allowed to reveal labels it acquires. The three datasets share one
short calibration corpus, the 51-row tests yield coarse AUC resolution, and the
policy uses simple Bernoulli uncertainty without diversity. Earliest threshold
crossing can reward a transient high AUC on a non-monotonic curve; AULC is reported
alongside it for that reason. The Qwen saving is a promising model-specific result,
not evidence of broad active-learning reduction.
