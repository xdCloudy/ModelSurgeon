# v0.8 Q4 learned versus magnitude and random pruning evidence

## Result

At an equal held-out budget of 10 channels, the gradient-profile LightGBM selected
zero constraint violations on all three models. Magnitude and random rankings
selected unsafe masks 10.5–67% of the time across their hierarchical intervals.
The learned selections also had lower mean measured ΔPPL than both baselines.

Paired mean-ΔPPL reductions versus magnitude were 0.03111
(0.02732–0.03484), 0.00830 (0.00298–0.01313), and 0.00250
(0.00114–0.00380). Reductions versus random were 0.03201
(0.02730–0.03666), 0.01847 (0.01362–0.02378), and 0.00343
(0.00181–0.00503). All intervals favor the learned ranker.

## Equal-budget protocol

- Each method ranks the same 51 component-held-out candidates and selects exactly
  10 (19.6% of the held-out pool). The safe constraint remains ΔPPL <= 0.01.
- The learned method ranks descending deterministic LightGBM safe probability using
  the Q3 static-plus-activation-plus-gradient profile. Magnitude ranks ascending
  mean absolute coupled-channel weight (`weight_l1_norm / weight_count`). Random
  uses a seeded permutation.
- Seeds 0–19 are paired across learned and random runs. The deterministic LightGBM
  and magnitude selections are intentionally stable across those seeds; random is
  not. Every persisted selection lists its exact example IDs.
- Confidence intervals use a two-level bootstrap: paired seeds are resampled and
  the 10 selected component outcomes within each chosen seed are resampled. Paired
  gain intervals apply the same seed draw to learned and baseline selections.
- A violation is a selected candidate whose measured single-mask ΔPPL exceeds
  0.01. Quality is the mean measured single-mask ΔPPL of the selected set; lower is
  better.

The per-channel coupled parameter proxies are 1,728, 2,880, and 2,688 parameters,
so all methods compare the same 17,280, 28,800, and 26,880-parameter selection
proxies respectively. These proxies describe equal structural cost within a model;
no cross-model claim depends on their absolute equality.

## Validity boundary

This is an equal-budget ranking study over independently measured single-channel
masks. It does not claim that applying all 10 channels simultaneously yields the
sum or mean of their individual ΔPPL values; channel interactions and cumulative
compression are unmeasured here. Q8 is the matched iterative-versus-one-shot study
that must evaluate combined mutations. The same within-calibration limitation from
Q3 also applies to the gradient ranker.
