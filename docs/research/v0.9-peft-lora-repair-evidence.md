## Validity boundary

This verifies PEFT injection, per-step held-out selection, deterministic adapter output, safetensors export, telemetry, and unchanged base weights on one real checkpoint and one linear module. The candidate was not structurally pruned; the one training example and one held-out text do not demonstrate recovery after damage or generalization across a task set. This run does not establish improved downstream quality, model compression, latency, or superiority to the previous implementation. A matched post-surgery study with a no-repair baseline and a broader held-out set remains necessary.
