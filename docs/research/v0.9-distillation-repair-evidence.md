# v0.9 logit-distillation repair evidence

## Result

A revision-pinned SmolLM2-135M baseline produced immutable logits for an 11-token
repair example. Its layer-0 input-normalization vector was then scaled to 90% to
form a distinct candidate. Two seeded distillation steps trained only that
576-parameter vector and reduced the complete 75% KL / 25% supervised objective
from 1.94234 to 1.92328. The best state was accepted with a content-derived child
checkpoint ID.

The source, perturbed-candidate, and repaired parameter SHA-256 values were,
respectively,
`a23ea2fd5797215a9e3cb70d7b62bbe2ab24aed12f606fd51e8d5e2b79c780ff`,
`5ca2efa82ac12901887c979591de96f51d4bb63df98a152dff54dc230fabca0f`, and
`b6e585ae1b002503f367afd22e6893255ec697c8ffb38a08317c574159d4ee5b`.
The distinct repaired digest confirms that the accepted trained state was retained.

## Compatibility, bounds, and resources

- Model: `HuggingFaceTB/SmolLM2-135M` at commit
  `93efa2f097d58c2a74874c7e644dbc9b0cee75a2`.
- Tokenizer: 49,152 effective entries, vocabulary SHA-256
  `d59adeae1bb1c383e51bcf30fd8ad05a2b82f7e752f72106f0f94c324cdb44b6`.
- Teacher logits: immutable FP32 CPU clone, 2,162,688 bytes, SHA-256
  `1d3cb85883afe5d95ebbcdb2b33b0af502c601b45cfef3100d7198169ef46ad0`.
- Temperature 2.0; distillation/supervised weights 0.75/0.25; learning rate
  0.001; two steps; seed 42.
- Training used FP32 on the RTX 3060. Peak allocated/reserved CUDA memory was
  587,401,216/595,591,168 bytes, peak RSS was 1,750,212,608 bytes, and measured
  repair wall time was 0.7671 seconds (0.0023 seconds immutable-logit capture and
  0.5963 seconds bounded training).

Unit coverage additionally proves distinct-teacher inference restores teacher
mode and leaves teacher weights trainable and unchanged, tokenizer mismatch fails
before candidate mutation, mixed loss is recorded, and wall exhaustion restores
the candidate exactly.

## Validity boundary

This smoke run proves immutable-logit capture, exact tokenizer compatibility,
bounded mixed-loss training, cost capture, candidate mutation, and safe lineage on
one model and repair example. It does not establish downstream generalization or
comparative repair benefit; those require the later held-out iterative study.
