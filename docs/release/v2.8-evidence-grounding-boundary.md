# ModelSurgeon v2.8 evidence-grounded explanations release boundary

Status: **closed by issue #480**. The versioned evidence record is
[`v2.8-evidence-grounding-release-v1.json`](../research/v2.8-evidence-grounding-release-v1.json).
This is a bounded explanation release, not a claim that the conversational
layer is a general optimizer or proof system.

## Release surface

The supported path is the typed, read-only chain:

```text
canonical campaign state/evidence
  → EvidenceSnapshot
  → EvidenceQuery / direct_evidence_report
  → typed canonical report
  → claim, negative-evidence, or measured-Pareto explanation
```

The query response, source-model digest, evidence IDs, provenance, outcome,
artifact lineage, measurements, units, uncertainty, missing fields, resource
usage, and deterministic identities remain authoritative. Direct evidence and
report APIs are the canonical source; conversational text is only a bounded
presentation of those records.

Claim rendering accepts a typed `EvidenceQueryResponse` only and revalidates
the exact canonical query projection. Negative-evidence direct and explanation
paths share the same response. Pareto direct, chat, and HTML renderings share
one typed selection explanation and replay the measured selection. Tampered or
stale snapshots, digest drift, unsupported inputs, unavailable fields, and
resource overruns fail closed.

## Claim and evidence policy

Every factual claim is source-traceable through an evidence ID and source
digest, or is explicitly unavailable. Measured values retain units and typed
uncertainty. An accepted decision with no canonical measurement is
prediction-only, not measured. Rejected, rolled-back, failed, unsupported,
unknown, and inconclusive outcomes remain retained and distinguishable.

The v2.8 boundary makes no claim of optimizer optimality, optimizer proof,
general correctness proof, live provider or external model quality, or any
measurement absent from the canonical evidence envelope. Unknown means that
retained evidence is incomplete or unobserved; it is not rejection or success.

## Factuality decision

The checked-in protocol retains six cases, three replay seeds, three methods,
and 54 cells. The grounded renderer and template-only baseline meet the strict
thresholds for source precision/recall, source-ID correctness,
negative-evidence coverage, uncertainty disclosure/calibration, and
deterministic IDs; measured-vs-predicted confusion, unsupported-claim rate,
and critical escapes are zero. The unconstrained text method remains a
retained negative control and is never shippable. Failed and inconclusive
cells remain in the canonical study record.

This decision is limited to the checked-in provider-independent corpus and
bounded renderer. It is not general language-model factuality, live provider
quality, or optimizer-quality evidence.

## Operational limitations and deferred types

Supported output is limited to claims projected from verified canonical
records. Unsupported claims include provider/transcript narrative as evidence,
optimizer proofs, unavailable measurements, live external quality, and
unsupported generalization. Unknown output covers missing measurements,
missing lineage, incomplete qualification, unobserved quality/latency/memory/
throughput/hardware, empty feasible frontiers, and incomplete execution.

Deferred explanation types are optimizer optimality/proof, live provider or
external-model quality, unmeasured hardware or latency, unbounded free-form
narrative, cross-model generalization without held-out evidence, and causal
attribution without a canonical intervention record.

Issue #420 remains the offline explorer authority, #411 the signed immutable
evidence-package authority, and #429 the measured benchmark/reference-artifact
authority. The v2.8 explanation boundary consumes these authorities without
duplicating them.
